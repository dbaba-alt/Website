At a service

Each service follows the same shape. Anyone interested in the teachings is invited to take part.

Sutra chanting
The service includes chanting from the Shin Buddhist tradition.
Recitations
Shared recitations are part of each service.
Dharma message
A talk about Shin Buddhism, given by the minister.

New to the temple? Write or call before your first visit and Reverend Baba will answer your questions.
